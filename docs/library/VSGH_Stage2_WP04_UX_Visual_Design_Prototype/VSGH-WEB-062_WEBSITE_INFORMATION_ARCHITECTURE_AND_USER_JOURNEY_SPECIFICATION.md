# VSGH-WEB-062 — Information Architecture and User Journey Specification

> **IA TREE SUPERSEDED BY: VSGH-WEB-081.** Section 2 below is historical. Audiences, journey principles, and CTA hierarchy remain in force unless later superseded.

## 1. Primary Audiences

The public website shall serve:
- Aerospace and defense organizations
- Materials and manufacturing partners
- Industrial customers
- Research institutions
- Investors and strategic partners
- Suppliers
- Prospective employees
- Government/industry stakeholders
- General technical audiences

## 2. Primary Information Architecture

```text
Home
├── About
│   ├── Company
│   ├── Vision & Mission
│   ├── Leadership
│   └── Facilities
├── Technology
│   ├── Advanced Materials
│   ├── Resource Recovery
│   ├── Metallurgy
│   ├── Processing
│   └── Applications
├── Aerospace & Advanced Applications
├── R&D
│   ├── Research Areas
│   ├── Publications
│   └── Technology Development
├── Sustainability
├── News / Insights
├── Careers
└── Contact
```

## 3. User Journey Principles

Every major audience shall reach a relevant proof point and CTA within a small number of interactions.

## 4. Primary Journeys

### Customer
Home → Technology → Capability → Application → Contact

### Research Partner
Home → R&D → Research Area → Collaboration → Contact

### Investor/Strategic Partner
Home → Company → Technology → Capabilities → Contact

### Candidate
Home → Careers → Opportunity → Application

### General Visitor
Home → About/Technology → Evidence → Contact

## 5. Navigation

Primary navigation shall remain concise and predictable.

## 6. CTA Hierarchy

Primary CTAs shall be reserved for:
- Discuss a Technology
- Explore Capabilities
- Partner With VSGH
- Contact VSGH
- Careers

Secondary CTAs shall support deeper exploration.
